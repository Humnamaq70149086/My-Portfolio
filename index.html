The Legal Desk

An app that helps people in Pakistan hire verified lawyers without getting scammed, with an AI assistant that answers legal questions. I built the retrieval-augmented generation layer: an in-app BM25 search over legal documents feeding Google Gemini, so answers rest on real sources instead of invented cases.

  • Visited the High Court to collect real legal documents for the corpus
  • Tested the pipeline against real and deliberately fake documents

About me

I'm in the final year of BS Information Engineering Technology at the University of Lahore, graduating in May 2027. I like problems where software has to be trustworthy: systems that can't be broken into easily, and AI that doesn't make things up.

That's what pulled me toward AI safety. Building RAG for The Legal Desk showed me how confidently a model can invent a court case, and how much careful retrieval and testing it takes to stop that. I want to keep working on hallucination, evaluation and AI governance, and help grow that research in Pakistan.
